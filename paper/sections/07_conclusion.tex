\section{Conclusion}

% claims: C01 C08 C10 C11 C13; sources: S01 S02 S10 S14 S15 S16 S17
We showed that, in one selected engineered model, an authenticated causal state
can select a statistical signal carried inside constrained generated text even
when the visible task and primitive randomness are matched. The public
receipt-bound integration was prospectively validated on 128/128 held-out
public closures. Protected qualification was not earned: one of 128 closures
failed computation fidelity, the descriptive result was 127/128, and final
protected confirmation was not run. The result is a bounded composition of
causal transport, trusted receipts, and statistical carriage---not output-only
authentication or general provenance for reasoning.
