\subsection{Registered denominators and gates}
% claims: C08 C10 C11; sources: S10 S11 S14 S16 S17
Public \intvone{} comprised 128 closures, 256 natural/alias execution packages, 1,280 receipt envelopes and generation authorizations, 10,240 outputs, 501,760 model steps, 245,760 eligible positions, 163,840 primitive authority scores, 20,480 aggregate scores, and 1,024 attack packages. Protected \intqone{} used the same registered denominators. Protected \intcone{} registered 256 closures but produced no executions, receipts, outputs, or decisions.

% claims: C08; sources: S10 S11
A complete closure required both sides to pass computation, exact receipt, abstract receipt, chain, semantic, threshold, unique-rank, strict-margin, carrier-off, wrong-authority, and attack gates. Failures could not compensate for one another.

The post hoc Wilson intervals in the main paper were deterministically computed from the frozen closure counts with $z=1.96$:
\begin{equation}
\frac{\hat p+z^2/(2n)\ \pm\ z\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n+z^2/(4n^2)}}{1+z^2/n}.
\end{equation}
They were not preregistered endpoints and do not assert that closures are population-representative.

\subsection{Training and channel details}

Model 1 used AdamW with initial learning rate $10^{-3}$, decay to $5\times10^{-5}$ after 750 warm-up steps, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size 256, gradient clipping at 1.0, and a maximum of 10,000 CPU float32 steps. The realiser used AdamW at 0.003, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size 64, ten epochs, and seed 817331. Its data contained 320 training, 96 calibration, and 96 untouched channel-qualification semantic objects, each with eight training variants.

\subsection{Protected-Q1 interpretation}
\label{app:q1}
% claims: C10; sources: S14 S15
The protected release, chronology, denominator, raw-evidence commitment, and reconstruction were valid. Scientifically, one closure failed computation fidelity and caused two dependent attack-expectation failures. Its root cause cannot be diagnosed or repaired from the closed evidence. The frozen adjudicator had no scientific-negative terminal and therefore emitted an implementation-or-evidence-invalid terminal. Both facts are preserved: \intqone{} did not qualify, and the historical terminal remains unchanged.
