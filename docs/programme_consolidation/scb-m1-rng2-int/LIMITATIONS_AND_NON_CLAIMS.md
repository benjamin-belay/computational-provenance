# Limitations and non-claims

These limitations must accompany any abstract, paper, presentation, or press
summary of the result.

1. **Selected model.** The end-to-end result is for prospectively designated
   Model 1. The earlier three-seed robustness programme failed validly at 2/3.
2. **Engineered bottleneck.** The causal state is an explicitly supervised,
   mandatory hard category. The experiment validates causal transport through
   this architecture; it does not discover an unknown pathway in an
   unconstrained model.
3. **Trusted receipts.** Exact and abstract claims rely on trusted HMAC
   authorities, canonical schemas, and receipt/chain verifiers. This is not
   public-key or trust-free attestation.
4. **Engineered realiser.** RNG2 is a purpose-built, position-indexed
   stochastic realiser. The result is not evidence that ordinary language
   models naturally emit this carrier.
5. **Not output-only provenance.** A statistical score is not sufficient by
   itself. Authenticated receipt and causal-chain evidence are required for
   the integrated provenance claim.
6. **Not chain-of-thought completeness.** The programme authenticates
   registered hard `z2` and `z3` events and their answer relation, not every
   internal computation or a complete reasoning trace.
7. **No protected confirmation.** Protected qualification was not earned.
   The descriptive result was 127/128, and the 256-closure confirmation role
   was not opened.
8. **No pooled denominator.** Public INT-V1 and protected INT-Q1 answer
   different prospective questions and must never be pooled.
9. **No post-hoc Q1 repair.** The frozen Q1 terminal and its terminal-ontology
   defect remain historical facts. The run cannot be relabelled as a pass.
10. **“SynthID-style” is narrow.** It denotes statistical signal carriage in
    generated output. It does not claim equivalence to, compatibility with, or
    evaluation of any production SynthID system.
11. **No superiority over sidecar HMACs.** A sidecar HMAC is simpler and
    cryptographically stronger for authenticating declared metadata. The
    contribution here is linking an authenticated causal-state class to a
    statistical property of generated output.
12. **Bounded task and ontology.** Results are limited to the registered task,
    state ontology, checkpoint, intervention rules, semantic payload, attack
    manifest, and detector operating point.
